# Roadside Support Guidance

If a vehicle becomes disabled, move it away from traffic when this can be done safely. Switch on the hazard lights, stop in a visible location, and keep occupants away from moving traffic.

When requesting roadside assistance, provide the vehicle location, a short description of the problem, and whether towing may be required. Do not attempt a repair in an unsafe location.

For a collision, fire, medical emergency, or immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.
