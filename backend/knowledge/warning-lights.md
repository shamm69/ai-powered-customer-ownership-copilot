# Dashboard Warning Lights

A red warning light can indicate a condition that needs immediate attention. Stop when it is safe, switch off the vehicle if continued operation may cause harm, and seek qualified assistance.

An amber or yellow warning light usually means the vehicle should be checked soon. Driving behavior, unusual sounds, smoke, overheating, or loss of braking can make the situation more urgent regardless of light color.

A warning light identifies a system that needs attention; it does not by itself provide a complete diagnosis.
