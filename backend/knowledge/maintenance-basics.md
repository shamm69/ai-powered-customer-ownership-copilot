# Scheduled Maintenance Basics

Scheduled maintenance uses both distance and time intervals. A vehicle may need service when either interval is reached, even if the other interval is not yet due.

Owners should keep dated service records with odometer readings and follow the service interval supplied for their vehicle. Common routine checks include engine oil, coolant, brake fluid, tires, lights, and windshield washers.

If a fluid leak, unusual noise, or sudden change in vehicle behavior appears, arrange an inspection rather than waiting for the next routine interval.
